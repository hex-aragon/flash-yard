# Flash Yard
A compact Korean shipping yard at late afternoon. Playful military training, original art, no copied Sudden Attack assets. Blue shipping containers and coral equipment, amber sunlight, pale sky, concrete floor with freight markings. Tactical orange versus naval blue training suits. The live map is the full-screen lobby background; left-aligned oversized condensed title and a compact frosted navy room panel on the right. No marketing sections. Native Korean system typography, Impact/Arial Narrow for the game wordmark, tabular system numerals for HUD. Palette: harbor #397b91, navy #172e40, coral #ef6c48, ivory #eef3ed, sunlight #f4cc80, concrete #aab4b4. Motion comes from the living 3D world. Controls should be understandable without technical implementation terms.
